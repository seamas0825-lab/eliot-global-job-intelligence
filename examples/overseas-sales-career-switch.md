# Example: Operations Candidate Switching to Overseas Sales

## Do not rename operations as sales

Candidate evidence:

- researched overseas partners;
- wrote English outreach;
- coordinated meetings and follow-up;
- maintained a tracker;
- did not own discovery, proposal, negotiation, or closed revenue.

## Mapping

| Operating step | Evidence | State |
| --- | --- | --- |
| account research | direct | VERIFIED |
| contact prioritization | partial | VERIFIED/PARTICIPATED |
| English outreach | direct | VERIFIED |
| discovery | observed/assisted | LEARNING GAP |
| negotiation | none | DO NOT CLAIM as experience |
| closed revenue | none | DO NOT CLAIM |

## Positioning

“I already have evidence for the front of the outbound process—research, prioritization, English communication, and disciplined follow-up. I have not independently owned commercial discovery or closing. I am positioning for a role where I can apply those mechanics and build supervised commercial ownership, not claiming a revenue record I do not have.”

## Work sample

Build a ten-account ICP hypothesis list with direct public triggers and a discovery map. Do not invent decision-maker emails, budgets, or purchase intent.
