# Example: China Social Operator Applying to Europe B2B SaaS Social

## Role hypotheses

- H1: content-led brand and category education;
- H2: sales-enablement content that creates qualified demand;
- H3: founder-led thought leadership and community credibility.

Keep all three until company accounts, cases, funnel ownership, and hiring-manager evidence distinguish them.

## Local Market Gate

“Europe” is provisional. Determine priority countries, business language, buyer roles, data/privacy constraints, and whether distribution centers on LinkedIn, communities, search, events, or partners.

## Sample protocol

- 5 direct B2B SaaS accounts in named countries;
- 5 analogous accounts that explain complex products through proof, cases, or practitioner education;
- 10–20 posts mapped to awareness, problem education, proof, and sales handoff.

## Candidate mapping

Transferable: editorial research, content system, production coordination, audience-comment analysis, funnel diagnosis.

Gap: native market vocabulary, B2B buying committee, pipeline attribution, local examples.

Do not say “I know the European market” from English-language desk research. Say the mechanism that transfers, the country-specific validation performed, and the remaining local check.
