# Example: US TikTok Creator BD Without Direct Creator-campaign Ownership

This example shows state transitions; names and numbers are placeholders, not market facts.

## Intake

- JD: US TikTok creator sourcing, outreach, negotiation, content follow-up, affiliate performance.
- Candidate: recruited overseas on-camera contributors and operated social accounts; no commission negotiation or GMV attribution.

## Role model

Primary family: `creator`, high confidence.

```text
affiliate revenue/content supply → creator persona → sourcing → qualification
→ outreach → negotiation → brief → publish → attribute → pay → renew
```

Unknowns: product margin, sample budget, flat-fee policy, usage-rights owner, and attribution window.

## Benchmark layer before creator sampling

Inspect 3–5 direct phone-accessory brands, 2–3 premium-accessory or affiliate analogues, and several recent creator campaigns. Reconstruct how at least one brand connects creator selection, collaboration model, proof style, affiliate/shop/DTC path, paid usage, measurement, and renewal. Use individual creators only after the brand-level mechanism is clear.

If current discovery/CRM/affiliate tools are material, use Grok for recent practitioner and tool leads, Perplexity for comparable brands and Reddit discussion, Gemini only if the category/market structure remains uncertain, and GPT for final evidence and answer review. Do not call all services with the same prompt.

## Stress-test repair

Weak: “I independently managed overseas influencer campaigns.”

### 可以直接说

“我之前招募过英语出镜人，主要负责画像、找人、英文沟通、筛选和后续推进。这段经历让我对怎么判断人选是否合适、怎么把沟通推进到下一步比较熟悉。达人佣金和 GMV 归因我还没有独立负责过，所以如果进入这个岗位，我会先从名单质量和外联推进做起，再在主管把关下补齐商务和归因。”

### COACH NOTES

Contributor recruitment is transferable to sourcing and qualification. Commission negotiation and GMV attribution remain learning gaps. Do not call the past work creator-campaign ownership.

## Prospective sample

Create a public ten-creator protocol, but publish only qualified rows. Label price, demographics, and sales as unknown unless observed. Add five questions the company must answer before outreach.
