# Example: China Inbound-travel Social Role

## Business model before content

Determine whether the company sells group tours, custom trips, local services, or consultation. Identify target countries, traveler type, price/risk level, inquiry channel, and sales handoff.

## Local query map

Use destination and decision language, not only competitor names:

```text
China itinerary; first time in China; China travel mistakes;
China payment guide; China private tour; is China safe to visit;
China vs Japan trip; Beijing itinerary; Yangshuo worth it
```

Translate or localize these for each source market. Map them to inspiration, planning, risk reduction, proof, and purchase.

## Mechanism analogies

Study destination brands outside China when they solve the same trust, complexity, or consultation problem. Do not copy their scenery or aesthetic; extract proof structure, itinerary simplification, local authority, and consultation handoff.

## KPI boundary

Views are an attention signal. The role may require inquiry quality, consultation booking, proposal progression, or booked trip value. Attribute only what the tracking chain supports.
