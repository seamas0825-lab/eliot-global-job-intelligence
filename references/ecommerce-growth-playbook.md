# Cross-border Ecommerce and Affiliate Growth Playbook

Use when the role owns some portion of store, listing, creative, affiliate, advertising, conversion, fulfillment, review, or retention performance.

## Operating chain

```text
Product economics → market demand → listing/storefront → creative
→ creator/affiliate supply → paid amplification → conversion
→ fulfillment → reviews → retention
```

First locate the role's actual segment. “Ecommerce operations” is not one job. Record which team owns price, inventory, media budget, creative, creators, customer service, logistics, attribution, and retention.

## Product-economics questions

- price, margin range if public/supplied, discount and commission constraints;
- category buying cycle, repeat potential, return/refund risk;
- shipping time, stock, sample availability, and geographic restrictions;
- proof required to overcome buyer risk;
- platform fees, promotion, and attribution limitations.

Do not estimate confidential margin, GMV, ROAS, or conversion rate from public appearances.

## Sample protocol

Choose the object that matches the role:

- 10 listings for merchandising/conversion;
- 10 creator posts for affiliate supply;
- 10 ads for creative/paid growth;
- 5 competitor stores for operating-system comparison.

Record direct URL/date, market, product/offer, proof, price signal, review signal, creative mechanism, CTA, conversion path, fulfillment/reputation risk, visible metrics, inference, and transfer lesson.

## Diagnostic map

Separate demand, traffic quality, listing clarity, proof, offer, checkout, stock, shipping, reviews, creator fit, paid amplification, attribution, and repeat purchase. A weak GMV result cannot be assigned to content without downstream evidence.

## Candidate mapping

Content or community experience can prove creative research, production, audience insight, and iteration. It cannot prove store ownership, GMV attribution, media buying, or fulfillment unless the candidate has direct evidence. Mark those as learning gaps and build a prospective diagnostic sample instead.
