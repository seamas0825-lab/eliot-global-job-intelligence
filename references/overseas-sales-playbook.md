# Overseas Sales, SDR, BD, and Channel Playbook

Use when the operating object is a target account, buyer contact, opportunity, partner, or pipeline stage.

## Operating chain

```text
ICP → buying trigger → account list → contact enrichment → prioritization
→ outreach → qualification → discovery → proposal/demo → follow-up
→ CRM → forecast → expansion
```

Determine territory, sales motion, average deal complexity, inbound/outbound mix, buyer roles, sales cycle, proof available, and whether the role owns sourced pipeline, closed revenue, partnerships, or only meeting creation.

## Sample protocol

Default set:

- 10 target companies that meet explicit ICP criteria;
- 5 buyer or influencer roles across the decision chain;
- 3–5 direct competitors and their public proof/cases;
- public outreach or customer language only when the original source is inspectable.

For each account record firmographic fit, trigger, likely problem, buyer role, evidence, disqualifier, priority, public contact path, hypothesis, and next validation action. Do not invent email addresses, budgets, intent, tech stacks, or authority.

## Discovery map

Prepare questions around:

- current process and trigger;
- consequence and priority;
- stakeholders and decision criteria;
- prior attempts and alternatives;
- timing, budget process, risk, and proof required;
- next step and mutual commitment.

## Candidate truth

Do not translate operations into “sales” unless the candidate actually qualified, persuaded, negotiated, or advanced a commercial decision. Distinguish:

- generated or handled leads;
- booked or joined meetings;
- influenced a proposal;
- supported negotiation;
- independently closed revenue;
- expanded or retained an account.

An operator may have transferable prospect research, English communication, process management, objection handling, or analytics. Name the mechanism and the missing commercial ownership.

## Interview probes

- What was your personal pipeline or revenue responsibility?
- How was the target list built and what disqualified an account?
- Which outreach message changed behavior and why?
- How did you qualify need, authority, timing, and next step?
- Where is the CRM or evidence trail?
- What happened when prospects did not respond or the forecast slipped?
