# Overseas Social and Content Playbook

Use when the role owns content, account, audience, community interaction, profile conversion, or social-led leads.

## Operating chain

```text
Business objective → audience tension → channel role → topic discovery
→ production → publishing → community interaction → profile conversion
→ lead/product path → analytics → iteration
```

Determine whether the real job optimizes awareness, trust, education, community, lead generation, product activation, or sales support. Do not equate posting volume with the business outcome.

## Sample protocol

Default:

- 5 direct competitors or category accounts;
- 5 mechanism-analogous accounts;
- 10–20 representative posts across funnel stages.

Record market/language, account role, audience, first line/frame, tension, visible proof, engagement quality, follow reason, CTA, conversion path, repeatable structure, production cost, observed public signals, risk, and direct URL/date.

## Search language

Generate local platform queries from:

```text
category + user problem + desired result + comparison + mistake + review
+ local slang + how-to + worth-it + before-you + things-I-wish-I-knew
```

Map queries to discovery, planning, risk reduction, proof, and purchase. Product-name search alone misses the audience's real language.

## Diagnosis

Separate:

- opening/attention failure;
- weak payoff or proof;
- poor audience-product fit;
- profile promise failure;
- CTA or handoff failure;
- downstream sales/product conversion failure;
- cultural or credibility rejection.

Do not claim that high views created high-intent demand without a traceable conversion event.

## Candidate transfer

Chinese social experience may prove research discipline, production systems, editorial judgment, community handling, or funnel thinking. It does not automatically prove local cultural fluency, platform distribution knowledge, or native copy. State the adaptation mechanism and validation plan.
