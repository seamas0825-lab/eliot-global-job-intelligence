# Work-sample Generation

Create a work sample only when it demonstrates role understanding without fabricating past experience or confidential access.

## Suitable samples

| Role | Safe prospective artifact |
| --- | --- |
| Creator/KOL BD | public creator shortlist, qualification rubric, outreach hypothesis, sample brief |
| Social/content | account audit, local query map, 3-post concept set, measurement plan |
| Sales/BD | ICP definition, 10-account hypothesis list, discovery map, outreach rationale |
| Ecommerce/Affiliate | listing/creative audit, affiliate shortlist, funnel diagnosis, test plan |

## External presentation language

Use a business title and begin with the useful judgment. Do not put “for the interviewer,” “candidate,” “prospective work sample,” “not internal access,” “historical employment,” or similar audit language in the hero or opening copy. Put a short “Scope and assumptions” section at the end, explaining which externally observable signals informed the brief and which internal goals, resources, baselines, or constraints still need validation.

## Employer-facing format

Default to `ROLE_OPPORTUNITY_BRIEF.md` plus a generated `ROLE_OPPORTUNITY_BRIEF.html`. Keep the page skimmable in five minutes and visually separate observed facts, implications, the bounded proposal, measurement, and open questions.

Capture 2–6 decision-relevant original-page screenshots during research. Record them in `work/evidence-screenshots.json` and show each with a concise observation, source name, observation date, and direct URL. Never use an AI-answer screenshot as evidence.

Do not include the resume, coach notes, candidate-evidence records, audit IDs, prohibited claims, hidden contact data, browser chrome, login state, or private sources. Convert source IDs to ordinary named source links in the rendered page. Create a PPT only when the user asks for it and an appropriate presentation tool is available.

## Quality contract

- Link each conclusion to source or sample IDs.
- Separate observed facts from assumptions.
- State missing internal data and questions needed before execution.
- Avoid invented contacts, rates, budgets, margins, conversion, or private metrics.
- Use the target market's actual language and workflow.
- Include success, diagnostic, and stop signals.
- Keep the artifact small enough to explain in five minutes.

Do not give away a full unpaid operating plan when the hiring process only needs proof of thinking. Prefer a bounded sample that demonstrates judgment and leaves execution details for a paid role.
