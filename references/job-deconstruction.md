# Job Deconstruction

## Separate four layers of a JD

| Layer | Question | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Outcome | What business result is owned? | qualified pipeline, creator GMV, lead conversion |
| Task | What recurring action produces it? | source, qualify, brief, follow up, analyze |
| Interface | Who or what must coordinate? | sales, creative, logistics, legal, creators |
| Tool/keyword | What supports the task but is not the work itself? | CRM, Excel, English, TikTok |

Do not treat a long tool list as proof of role clarity.

## Infer hiring motivation cautiously

Use evidence such as product launch, market entry, account activity, job clusters, channel expansion, founder statements, funding, store growth, or obvious operational gaps. Record:

- verified business change;
- inferred problem;
- alternative explanation;
- evidence that would reverse the inference;
- interview question that can resolve it.

Do not infer distress, revenue, team structure, or strategy from a single absence or stale page.

## Role Reality Gate

Answer before drafting interview responses:

1. What result is the role accountable for?
2. What is the complete operating chain?
3. Which responsibilities are core versus occasional?
4. Which team owns inputs, approvals, and downstream conversion?
5. What budget, content, product, data, and tooling are assumed?
6. Which KPI is a leading signal and which is the actual outcome?
7. What contradictions or missing dependencies exist?

Gate outcomes:

- **PASS:** one coherent role model is supported.
- **PROVISIONAL:** two or more plausible models remain; keep them separate and prepare clarification questions.
- **BLOCKED:** the JD and company evidence cannot support a responsible interpretation; do not write confident role-specific answers.

## Common contradictions

- one person appears to own strategy, daily production, paid media, sales, fulfillment, and analytics;
- revenue responsibility without pricing, inventory, attribution, budget, or sales support;
- “local market expertise” while the target country and language are undefined;
- senior outcomes paired with entry-level authority or compensation;
- creator volume goals without product margin, samples, payment, usage rights, or approval capacity;
- sales goals without ICP, territory, proof, CRM, or lead source.

Turn each contradiction into a neutral interview question, not an accusation.
