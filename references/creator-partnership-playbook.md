# Creator, KOL, Affiliate, and Partnership Playbook

Use for roles whose operating object is a creator relationship or creator-generated asset.

## Operating chain

```text
Campaign objective → creator persona → sourcing → qualification → outreach
→ negotiation → briefing → content approval → publishing
→ usage rights/whitelisting → tracking → payment → renewal
```

First determine whether the company needs brand reach, content production, affiliate sales, paid amplification assets, shop livestreams, or a blended program. The same title has different economics and daily work in each model.

## Competitive benchmark before the creator list

Before collecting individual creators, inspect how 3–5 relevant brands in the target market structure creator marketing. Add 2–3 business-model or mechanism analogues when direct competitors reveal too little.

For each brand determine from public evidence:

- creator-program objective and visible creator mix;
- gifting, paid, affiliate, ambassador, shop, or hybrid model;
- repeated content hooks, proof, CTA, disclosure, and brand integration;
- whether organic creator content is reused in ads, whitelisted, connected to affiliate/shop/DTC, or retained as an ambassador system;
- visible sourcing/application route, campaign cadence, and renewal signals;
- what can be borrowed, what depends on different economics, and what remains unknown.

Reconstruct at least one full public-evidence chain. A ten-creator shortlist without brand/campaign context fails the Benchmark Intelligence Gate.

## Creator sample fields

Capture for each `SMP-CRE-*`:

- country, language, niche, audience hypothesis, platform, and public URL;
- follower band and observation date;
- recent content sample and median visible performance where directly observable;
- comment quality, suspected audience mismatch, commercial-content share, and posting stability;
- recurring content mechanism and proof style;
- brand/product fit and likely conversion path;
- public collaboration route;
- public pricing signal only when directly observed; otherwise `unknown`;
- suitability for affiliate, flat fee, gifting, ambassador, or paid usage;
- reuse value, brand-safety risk, fake-engagement indicators, and recommendation tier.

Never invent contact details, rates, demographics, sales, or audience quality. Public followers alone cannot qualify a creator.

## Tool-landscape decision

When the role requires platform/tool understanding, research current discovery, CRM/outreach, affiliate, gifting, contract/rights, payment, amplification, and measurement options. Compare advantages and constraints against the company's likely scale and workflow. Use practitioner discussion for discovery and official pages for capability verification; do not list products without a job-to-be-done comparison.

## Qualification logic

Evaluate separately:

1. audience-product fit;
2. credible content performance and stability;
3. natural integration quality;
4. ability to create reusable proof or ad assets;
5. commercial and operational fit;
6. disclosure, safety, rights, and reputation risk.

Avoid a single weighted score that hides a fatal veto. Use `priority`, `watch`, or `exclude` with reasons.

## Interview probes

- How do you build and prioritize a creator list?
- How do you distinguish reach from purchase intent?
- What information is needed before quoting or negotiating?
- How do you structure usage rights, exclusivity, revisions, and payment?
- How do you diagnose strong views but weak conversion?
- What changes when budget is cut or no creator responds for three weeks?

If the candidate recruited hosts, ambassadors, speakers, or community partners, map the shared sourcing/qualification/communication mechanism but do not call it independent creator campaign management unless that is true.
