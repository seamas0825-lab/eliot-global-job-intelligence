# AI Research Orchestration

Use AI only when it adds distinct source access, multilingual coverage, structured extraction, contradiction, or synthesis. Model agreement is not independent evidence.

## AI value gate

Before opening a branch ask whether the uncertainty is better resolved by:

- the company's original page or product;
- a native platform page or one real practitioner;
- candidate-supplied evidence;
- a clarification question in the interview;
- a small prospective sample;
- a reversible judgment call.

If yes, use that path. AI output is a lead until its original source is opened and supports the claim.

## Preferred named-service roles

Use these defaults when the service is currently accessible and the evidence gap matches. They are role assignments, not mandatory calls or permanent capability claims.

| Service | Primary job | Useful outputs | What it must not do |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Grok** | Current practitioner and expert intelligence | marketing-expert experience, recent cases, workflow debates, newly used tools, AI-driven marketing products, live X discussion | Treat expert opinion or an X thread as verified market prevalence or product capability |
| **Perplexity** | Source and benchmark discovery | comparable brands/accounts, public campaign leads, Reddit user/creator discussions, source-linked case discovery | Treat its synthesis as evidence without opening originals |
| **Gemini Deep Research** | Industry and market structure | category landscape, multi-market patterns, competitive structure, macro direction, long-form research map | Replace country/platform-level behavioral evidence with a broad dossier |
| **GPT** | Integration, review, and editorial quality control | merge the evidence packet, detect contradictions, audit claim boundaries, review interview intent, remove robotic language, test completeness | Certify another AI's unsupported claim or become a fourth duplicate discovery branch |

The names above may be unavailable or change. Verify current service, account, mode, and editor through the browser capability gate. Use an equivalent capability when necessary and record the substitution.

For unstable tool landscapes, practitioner workflows, and “what is being used now,” default the Grok discovery window to the most recent six months unless the role requires another period. Require dated posts, named practitioners, direct links, and a short reason each lead can change the role model or work sample. Then verify product capabilities on official pages.

## Route by capability

| Capability | Suitable question | Verification |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Live social search | current discussion, founder view, creator language | recency, platform coverage, direct URLs |
| Source discovery | Reddit, media, cases, official pages | open cited originals |
| Deep research | multi-country structure, competitors, policy landscape | mode, completion, traceability |
| Long-context reasoning | JD decomposition, evidence mapping, red-team | source-to-claim links |
| Dynamic native browsing | TikTok, LinkedIn, Instagram, storefronts | browser gate and live page readback |
| Local-language reasoning | native queries and cultural meaning | original wording and translation risk |

Choose the smallest set of services with different evidence roles. Do not require named vendors. Do not send the same broad question to several models to manufacture consensus.

## Build the coverage plan

Before opening a service, write:

```text
Decision gap:
Why direct browsing is insufficient:
Selected service and distinct job:
Expected evidence leads:
Original sources that must be recovered:
Stop rule:
Why other services are omitted:
```

Examples:

- For a Creator BD role with weak competitor knowledge, use Perplexity for comparable-brand/account and Reddit discovery; use Grok if recent practitioner workflows or tool landscape could change the work sample; use Gemini only when category/market structure remains a first-order uncertainty; use GPT at convergence to review the assembled packet and candidate-ready answers.
- For a narrow social role with strong direct sources, use browser research plus GPT review; do not open four services merely because they exist.
- For a multi-country category-entry role, Gemini may own the macro branch while Perplexity and native browsing validate selected brands and communities.

## Branch contract

Save:

```text
Branch ID; capability; assigned question; dependency;
service/model/mode; capability verified date; limitation; fallback;
exact prompt; session/conversation ID or URL; source IDs;
facts recovered; hypotheses; contradictions; inclusion decision
```

The prompt must include the role decision, company, named market, language, time window, source requirements, exact output fields, uncertainty rules, and anti-fabrication requirement.

## Multi-round protocol

1. **Discovery:** bounded leads with direct URLs and dates.
2. **Interrogation:** missing evidence and what would falsify the synthesis.
3. **Contradiction:** reconcile supplied contrary evidence without averaging away disagreement.
4. **Extraction:** claim ledger with source, date, evidence/inference, confidence, and decision affected.

Stop when another round cannot change the role model, candidate claim, answer, or work sample.

## GPT final review contract

Give GPT the recovered source ledger, benchmark chain, candidate evidence map, and draft answers. Require it to check:

- missing competitor or mechanism coverage;
- evidence/inference confusion and shared-source duplication;
- interviewer intent and answer relevance;
- internal control language leaking into candidate speech;
- repeated disclaimers, generic AI phrasing, and claims that fail follow-up;
- whether the final brief and CSV support the spoken answers.

GPT may propose rewrites and gaps. It may not upgrade evidence status.

## Convergence gate

When two or more AI services are used:

1. deduplicate shared original URLs;
2. open material original sources;
3. classify each claim as `verified_consensus`, `verified_divergence`, `unverified_consensus`, or `single_branch_lead`;
4. preserve disagreements and retrieval limits;
5. exclude inaccessible or circular citations from evidence;
6. make the final decision from verified evidence, not vote count.
